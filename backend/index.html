About this prototype

DLSMirror has two modes, shown at the top of the screen.

Live mode (the default) runs a real reasoning engine as you use it, and it no longer stops asking questions after a fixed number \u2014 it keeps going until the evidence is actually sufficient: no unresolved contradiction, no critical piece of information missing, no important signal left unsupported, no untested relationship between multiple active signals. That decision is made by the app itself, not by the model.

Evidence has a real lifecycle. Every fact starts as owner-provided, observed, inferred, or a hypothesis \u2014 and it can only become verified through an explicit step the app controls (the owner confirming a targeted follow-up question), never because the model simply sounds confident. Each item keeps both your original wording and a normalized meaning, so nothing gets lost in translation or paraphrase.

Everything is traceable through a graph. Evidence, signals, relationships, patterns, the diagnosis, and the commercial thesis are connected nodes and edges, not separate paragraphs. Opening "Why DLSMirror thinks this" on the Diagnose screen walks that graph backward for you. Confidence is computed from how much and how independent the supporting evidence is \u2014 ten repeats of the same statement don't count as ten proofs.

Demo mode uses a small set of fixed, pre-written example situations instead \u2014 useful for a quick, offline walkthrough, but limited to a handful of common situations.

Continuous learning: once a decision is on file, you can record what actually happened. DLSMirror compares it to what was expected, names the difference and a possible explanation, and turns it into a new signal that reopens Discovery \u2014 nothing is deleted or restarted from zero, and older diagnoses are archived rather than erased when new evidence changes the picture.

Contradiction detection: if something you say later doesn't square with something you said earlier, DLSMirror flags it, keeps it open, and asks about it directly \u2014 it's never silently marked resolved just because the conversation moves on.

An important change from earlier prototypes: this browser never talks to Anthropic, or any reasoning provider, directly. Every reasoning call goes to this app's own backend at /api/reason, which holds the provider credential, builds the actual prompts, validates the structured result, and only then hands back clean data. Open your browser's network inspector \u2014 you'll see requests to /api/reason and nothing else.

For anyone curious what's happening underneath, there's a small "Debug" link in the top bar that shows the discovery assessment, the full evidence lifecycle, the relationship graph, and the diagnostic chain DLSMirror is building \u2014 meant for development, not for the everyday experience.
